Questions

Straight answers before you book.

Still unsure? Call (813) 428‑1017 and talk to a real person.

Can you really come today?

Most days, yes. Book before early afternoon and we aim to be there the same day. If we can't make it, we tell you right away so you're not left waiting.

How much will the repair cost?

We diagnose first, then give you the full price before any work starts. You approve it or you don't. No surprise fees on the invoice.

Will you try to sell me a new system?

No. We repair first. We only bring up a replacement when a repair truly costs more than it's worth, and we show you the numbers so you can decide.

What does the $89 tune up include?

We clean the coils and drain line, check refrigerant and electrical parts, and adjust the system so it runs quieter and uses less power.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Our Florida licence number is listed at the top of this page, and we're happy to show proof of insurance on arrival.

Which areas do you cover?

Tampa and nearby neighbourhoods including South Tampa, Westchase, Carrollwood, Brandon and Riverview. Not sure? Ask in the booking form.

What happens after I send the form?

We call you back to confirm the time window. Your details are only used for this request.
